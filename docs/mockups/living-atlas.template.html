A1 · Two registers — decided

Covers are pathos: footage from any well-licensed library (Pexels, Coverr, Commons, your own), chosen for feeling, credited top-left, graded toward the guide's accent. The ≈/⚠/Checked apparatus never blesses a cover — it belongs to the ledger below, which stays ethos: every price, hour, and route verified.

A2 · The morph stays cheap

The card→hero View Transition carries the poster; video cross-fades in ~300ms after arrival on the house ease. CLS: video is absolutely positioned, exactly like the photo.

A3 · Control

A visible pause (bottom-right) satisfies WCAG 2.2.2 and taste alike. Reduced-motion users never see motion at all — try the toggle: this mock obeys it.

A4 · Budget

Video never counts against the 200 KB first-paint budget; it's a lazy, hot-linked stream with a ~4 MB ceiling — nothing heavy enters the repo.

PLATE 05·B

Every section opens with an anchor

The guides are prose-heavy, and prose walls are where attention dies. Decided: every section opens with a visual anchor — a figure drawn from the guide's own data, plus a photo band where a good image exists. The figure is the section's thesis at a glance; the prose becomes the detail you drill into, not the wall you face. One gentle draw-in per section, first time it scrolls into view — then everything holds still.

THE WAY FORWARD

One programme, six moves

The three-path menu didn't earn its keep, so here's the honest version: a sequence, reorderable, with the foundation first — because every later surface inherits the type and the voice.

R1

Field type + the voice. Collapse to two faces site-wide (Literata via optical sizes + Source Sans 3 — self-hosted fontsource, same as today) and settle the labels: literal always, warmth in the descriptors. Zero JS, one session, and every screen changes temperature at once.

R2

Mobile goes native. Bottom bar (Journey · Today · Map · Kit), the journey sheet, the floating Today chip; the mobile pill strip retires. The critiqued pain dies first. SOS keeps its post.

R3

Desktop wears the horizon. The line replaces pills on desktop only, absorbs the reading-progress bar, keeps tablist semantics and the arrow-key ring.

R4

Living covers. The pathos layer as already decided — masthead first, then hub. Liberal sourcing, credited, accent-graded, gated on reduced-motion and Save-Data.

R5

The interior atlas + section anchors. Every section opens with its data-drawn figure (+ photo band where curated); leg headers from real pins; the editorial measure with the quiet numbers rail.

R6

The Composer. Unit facets into the schema, compose-guide.mjs beside the scaffolder (pure, tested), scaffold + research-pass integration for new guides, check-mode proposals for live ones. From then on, every guide's tabs assemble themselves.

Built for unattended execution

How Fable runs this without you in the room

⚙

Each R-phase is a self-contained session: entry = previous phase merged, gates green · work = the phase scope, nothing else · exit = the full ship loop (build, tests, a11y, perf budget, grep dist/) + commit + push + HANDOFF updated. A phase that can't pass its gates stops and reports — it never improvises past a red gate.

✋

Hard sign-off boundaries the machine may not cross: descriptor/title text on live guides (content), any live-guide recomposition (proposal-only), publishing footage choices, and re-recording a11y baselines anywhere but CI. Everything else inside a phase's scope is pre-authorized by your morning answers below.
