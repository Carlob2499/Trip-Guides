WaypointDesign Study Nº 12026-07-27

The Living Atlas

Five plates and three ways forward. The thesis: promote the signature you already own — the trip unfolds, day by day — from three appearances to every surface. Heroes become living covers; the pill toolbar becomes the route itself; hub, guide, and day become one map at different zooms. And the site speaks in two registers, deliberately split: covers court the heart — pathos, liberal — while the content keeps the ledger — ethos, verified. The shell may seduce; the spine may not lie.

Honest fallbacks

Video is an enhancement gated on reduced-motion, Save-Data, and in-view. First paint is the poster — today's photo — always.

A1 · Two registers — decided

Covers are pathos: footage from any well-licensed library (Pexels, Coverr, Commons, your own), chosen for feeling, credited top-left, graded toward the guide's accent. The ≈/⚠/Checked apparatus never blesses a cover — it belongs to the ledger below, which stays ethos: every price, hour, and route verified.

A2 · The morph stays cheap

The card→hero View Transition carries the poster; video cross-fades in ~300ms after arrival on the house ease. CLS: video is absolutely positioned, exactly like the photo.

A3 · Control

A visible pause (bottom-right) satisfies WCAG 2.2.2 and taste alike. Reduced-motion users never see motion at all — try the toggle: this mock obeys it.

A4 · Budget

Video never counts against the 200 KB first-paint budget; it's a lazy, hot-linked stream with a ~4 MB ceiling — nothing heavy enters the repo.

D1 · Latent structure, not ornament

The leg header (Daejeon → Busan → Seoul · KTX 10:44) is assembled from data every day entry already carries. Nothing is invented — the atlas look is the data made visible.

D2 · The editorial measure

Serif lead at ~62ch does the persuading; the mono rail holds the numbers, flags, and the verification stamp. Readers stop hunting through bold-spans for the price.

D3 · Honesty as a visual system

≈, ⚠, and Checked stamps stop being inline typography and become the rail's grammar — the colophon's signature, extended upward through the whole guide.

Five decisions gate the build

№1

Video sourcing — decided 2026-07-27. Covers are the pathos layer: any well-licensed cinematic footage, chosen for feeling, credited, graded toward the accent. The verification apparatus applies to content only — it never blesses a cover. ✓ liberal covers · rigorous content

№2

Expedition Line: replace or augment? Wholesale replacement is the drastic path; Korea at 375px is the test that decides it. rec · replace, with the augment fallback pre-agreed

№3

Video weight. ~4 MB Commons hot-links on hub and mastheads, or masthead-only first? Save-Data gates help but don't decide the default.

№4

Route-draw for pin-less guides. Ship with center-only map data, or contours-only until real pins exist? rec · contours until pins — honest ornament

№5

Phase order — which path above. rec · Path A, then fold into C
