- Traveler-only forks become question cards with the assumption used; research does not wait.
- Unsupported claims are downgraded, flagged, or omitted. Perishable and high-risk facts keep their
  evidence and freshness obligations.
- Stop after the semantic merge and artifact self-check. Do not run shell/node/npm, networked
  verification, commit, land, or publish.
